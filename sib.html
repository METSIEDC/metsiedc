CASE FILE 03
01 / MISSION

Find the startup. Find the truth.

Task 03 is an investigation-based startup challenge. Teams receive information progressively and must connect clues, identify the real business problem and make a founder-level decision. This is not a routine case study.

01

INVESTIGATE

Study each clue, document and signal instead of accepting it at face value.

02

ANALYSE

Compare evidence, spot contradictions and understand the business behind the clues.

03

DECIDE

Identify the actual threat and recommend what the founder should do next.

02 / EVIDENCE

Not every clue deserves your trust.

SIB may release evidence in different formats. Your job is to verify before you conclude.

GENUINE

Supported Evidence

Information backed by credible and verifiable sources.

MISLEADING

Wrong Direction

Information that may contain truth but can create a false conclusion without context.

COMPLETELY FALSE

Fabricated Evidence

Information designed to look credible but unsupported by facts.

04 / GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Think like a founder. Investigate like a detective.

TEAMWORK
Every member should contribute. Collaborate, discuss and document your reasoning.
VERIFY
Use credible sources. Do not treat appearance, numbers or a reliability score as proof.
NO GUESSWORK
Every major classification needs a logical reason and, where possible, supporting evidence.
INTEGRITY
No plagiarism, fake sources, manipulation of evidence or sharing confidential clues before release.

EVERY CLUE MATTERS.

Every idea counts. The truth is out there. Find it.
